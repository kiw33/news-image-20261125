## The six panels

1. **Ukraine claims Baltic ship strike** — President Volodymyr Zelenskiy
   confirmed Ukraine was responsible for Saturday's drone strike on a
   Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland; Norway, a strong supporter
   of Kyiv, urged caution in such operations. Finnish, Estonian and
   Russian vessels took part in a joint rescue operation to put out the
   fire; on Sunday a Russian vessel towed the ship back into Russian
   waters, where it is anchored in St Petersburg (Reuters world news
   summary, 25 Nov).
2. **Moscow warns of nuclear strike** — Former president Dmitry Medvedev,
   deputy chairman of Russia's Security Council, wrote in an article
   published Monday in the Moscow Journal of International Law that the
   massive use of non-nuclear means causing catastrophes makes a
   "preventive nuclear attack" by Russia possible, and that secret or
   hidden Western supplies of nuclear weapons to Kyiv would provoke one;
   he argued Ukraine and NATO allies have already created such
   circumstances (TASS excerpt, via NY Post, 24–25 Nov).
3. **NATO sends AWACS to Poland** — NATO is planning to deploy
   early-warning aircraft in Poland following reports of missiles
   landing in the country during an attack on Ukraine; the North Atlantic
   Council agreed the deployment on 23 Nov after Polish prosecutors
   confirmed all three objects that fell on 20 Nov were Russian Kh-101
   cruise missiles (25 Nov headlines; ISW / Argus Russia brief, 25 Nov).
4. **Houthis hit Riyadh's top tower** — Yemen's Houthi fighters struck
   the tallest tower in Riyadh on Tuesday for the second time in less
   than a week, wounding at least 11 people. Saudi Civil Defense
   reported the injuries and posted a picture of the landmark tower
   housing the headquarters of the Saudi Public Investment Fund
   (Reuters world news summary, 25 Nov).
5. **Huawei convicted in US trial** — A jury in New York City convicted
   Huawei on Tuesday in a sprawling US criminal case on charges that it
   deceived banks about its business in Iran and stole trade secrets
   from American companies. The case began in 2018; the US placed Huawei
   on a trade blacklist the following year (Reuters world news summary,
   25 Nov).
6. **CIA and ODNI hit by breach** — The US CIA and the Office of the
   Director of National Intelligence suffered a cybersecurity breach
   involving some unclassified information, a source told Reuters
   (Reuters, reported 25 Nov).
